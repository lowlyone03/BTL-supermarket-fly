VI SAO ADAPTER

THIET KE LUONG (GIU NGUYEN NGHIEP VU)
  1. TN them hang -> quote (KM, diem) nhu hien tai.
  2. POST /cashier/invoices -> HoaDon Nhap (neu chua co).
  3. Chon QR (co the hon hop: 1 dong TM + 1 dong QR).
  4. POST .../payments/qr
       SoTien = phan con lai (hoac so TN nhap, khong vuot con lai)
       INSERT ThanhToan:
         PhuongThuc = N'QR'
         TrangThai  = N'Cho xac nhan'
         SoTien, NgayTT=GETDATE(), NgayXacNhan=NULL
       Goi adapter.createPayment(...)
       Tra ve: MaTT, qrCode / paymentUrl, expiredAt
  5. Modal POS hien QR hoac mo URL sandbox + "Dang cho khach thanh toan".
     Poll 2s hoac SSE cho den Thanh cong / That bai / timeout.
  6. IPN / verifyCallback — KHONG TIN SO TIEN TU CALLBACK
       Thu tu BAT BUOC:
         a. Verify chu ky (checksum VNPay).
         b. Map MaThamChieuCong / orderId -> MaTT. Khong thay -> reject, khong update.
         c. Kiem tra amount callback = ThanhToan.SoTien (sai lech 0d).
            Vi du MaTT yeu cau 250.000, IPN 25.000: DU chu ky hop le
            VAN KHONG danh dau 250.000 thanh cong. Ghi log + That bai / Cho xu ly.
         d. Kiem tra trang thai gateway = paid / success.
         e. Kiem tra dong nay CHUA xu ly (TrangThai != Thanh cong).
            Idempotent: IPN lan 2 khong tru ton 2 lan
            (completeInvoice da co nhanh alreadyCompleted).
         f. Moi UPDATE ThanhToan Thanh cong + NgayXacNhan + MaGiaoDich.
         g. Neu tong Thanh cong = TongThanhToan: goi completeInvoice
            (tru ton, DonGiaVon, diem, audit UC25).
       Return URL chi de UX; QUYET DINH so lieu nam o IPN + buoc c.

  7. Timeout / khach khong tra / VNPay that bai:
       TrangThai = That bai. Khong tru ton. TN co the tao GD moi
       (MaGiaoDich moi, khong tai su dung ma cu).

  8. HUY / KHACH BO GIUA HON HOP (BAT BUOC — EDGE CASE)
       Vi du: HD 100.000; TM 80.000 Thanh cong; QR 20.000 That bai;
       khach: "Thoi em khong mua nua."
       HoaDon van Nhap. KHONG DUOC huy truc tiep / xoa HD.

       Rule:
         HoaDon da co >= 1 ThanhToan = Thanh cong
         -> cancelInvoice TRA VE 400.
         (Code hien tai salesController.cancelInvoice da CHAN neu co
          ThanhToan Thanh cong — GIU va BO SUNG luong hoan.)

       Neu khach huy:
         1. Hoan / dao TUNG dong Thanh cong (P1: hoan TM ghi so noi bo
            vao ket ca / TongTienHoanMat hoac dong ThanhToan Loai=Hoan;
            QR/The/CK: xac nhan tay, khong refund tu dong qua VNPay).
         2. Audit UC25: "Hoan truoc khi huy nhap MaTT... so tien..."
         3. Khi tong Thanh cong con lai = 0 (cac dong cu van CON, khong xoa)
         4. Moi cho Huy hoa don nhap.

       Cau do an (neu can rut gon slide):
         "Neu thanh toan hon hop da co mot phan Thanh cong, Thu ngan
          bat buoc hoan tat phan con lai HOAC lam quy trinh hoan tien
          truoc khi huy hoa don."

       Dong ca: van chan neu con Cho xac nhan. Neu con Thanh cong tren
       HD Nhap: cung khong cho dong ca (bo sung check: HoaDon Nhap
       khong duoc phep con tien Thanh cong — phai hoan hoac complete).

  9. Fallback: van giu dong QR thu cong (nhap MaGiaoDich + tick Thanh cong)
     de demo mat mang / sandbox chet.
 10. Dong ca: neu con Cho xac nhan -> 400 nhu hien tai.
