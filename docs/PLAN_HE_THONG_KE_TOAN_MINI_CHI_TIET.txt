5.1. Ban hang — tien mat / QR / the / CK / hon hop + VAT dau ra
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Dieu kien
  HoaDon.TrangThai = N'Hoan thanh'
  Tong ThanhToan.TrangThai=N'Thanh cong' = HoaDon.TongThanhToan
  (P0 da chan tra thieu).

Tach thue TREN DONG (sau KM va diem)

  ThanhTienSauGiam dong
    = phan bo TongThanhToan theo ty trong ThanhTien dong
      SAU khi tru TienGiamGia + TienDiemQuyDoi o header.
    Cach trien khai de xuat (tranh lech 1 dong):
      1. Tinh Tam = max(0, ThanhTien - GiamGiaDong).
      2. TyTrong = Tam / SUM(Tam). Neu SUM=0 thi ty trong deu.
      3. GiamChung = TienGiamGia + TienDiemQuyDoi (header).
      4. ThanhTienSauGiam_i = round( TongThanhToan * TyTrong ) o moi dong
         tru dong cuoi = phan con lai (de tong = TongThanhToan).
      5. t = ChiTietHoaDon.ThueSuat (copy tu SanPham luc ban, KHONG sua sau).
         HD moi hoan thanh: t BAT BUOC IN (0, 5, 8, 10), KHONG NULL.
         SanPham.ThueSuat NULL = QL chua chon -> POS chan add line / complete.
         t = 0: hop le (0%), TienThue_i = 0, DT_i = ThanhTienSauGiam_i.
         t NULL chi xay ra tren HD cu (truoc KT) — khong complete HD moi;
         quet ghi so HD cu ThueSuat NULL: KHONG post BAN_HANG, ChoGhiSo
         LyDo=THIEU_THUE (UC40 "Thieu thue").
      6. TienThue_i = round( ThanhTienSauGiam_i * t / (100 + t) )  neu t>0
                      = 0 neu t=0
      7. DT_i (511) = ThanhTienSauGiam_i - TienThue_i
      8. HoaDon.TienThue = SUM(TienThue_i)
      9. Kiem: SUM(DT_i) + SUM(TienThue_i) = TongThanhToan
         Neu lech 1 dong do lam tron: cong/tru vao DT dong lon nhat.

  1 HD 2 thue suat: 33311 van 1 TK, nhung bang ke UC40 TACH theo dong/suat.

But toan BAN_HANG (LoaiButToan='BAN_HANG', LoaiChungTu='HoaDon')

8.3. Can doi phat sinh (CDPS)  — UC38
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1 dong / 1 TK dang dung (ke ca du = 0 neu muon du COA).

  Cot:
    Ma TK | Ten TK
    Du dau No | Du dau Co
    PS No | PS Co
    Du cuoi No | Du cuoi Co

    SUM(PS No) = SUM(PS Co)
    SUM(Du cuoi No) = SUM(Du cuoi Co)
  Neu lech -> banner do, khong xuat "dat".

8.4. KQKD mini  — UC43  (TACH vs dong tien; PHAT SINH THUAN)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Nhan tren man: "Ket qua hoat dong ke toan (mini) — KHONG tru tien tra NCC".

  NGUYEN TAC (CHOT 1.2 — sua bug 1.1: KQKD chi lay 1 chieu PS):
    So cai dung net movement (goc + dao deu DaGhiSo, 100 + (-100) = 0).
    KQKD PHAI CUNG net. KHONG lay chi PS Co 511 / chi PS No 632.
    Vi du: BAN_HANG Co 511 100, dao No 511 100.
      SAI (1.1): PS Co 511 = 100  -> van hien DT 100.
      DUNG:     511 = PS Co − PS No = 0.

  Loc dong (QUERY — LIKE hop le o day, KHONG dung trong filtered index):
    ButToan.TrangThai = 'DaGhiSo'
    AND LoaiButToan NOT IN ('KET_CHUYEN', 'DAO_KET_CHUYEN', 'SODU_DAU_KY')
    -- GOM DAO_BAN_HANG, DAO_GIA_VON, DAO_DOI_TRA_*, ... de tu triet tieu.
    -- Loai ket chuyen: neu tinh KET_CHUYEN thi No 511 / Co 911 se nuot DT.
    -- Co the viet them AND LoaiButToan NOT LIKE N'DAO_KET_CHUYEN' — thua
    --   neu da co IN list, nhung QUERY thi LIKE duoc.

  Cong thuc phat sinh thuan (1 TK, 1 ky):
    511  = SUM(SoTienCo) − SUM(SoTienNo)     -- du Co
    711  = SUM(SoTienCo) − SUM(SoTienNo)     -- du Co
    5212 = SUM(SoTienNo) − SUM(SoTienCo)     -- du No
    632  = SUM(SoTienNo) − SUM(SoTienCo)
    642  = SUM(SoTienNo) − SUM(SoTienCo)

  Cot / dong (lay so cai, ky):

    1. Doanh thu ban hang          = 511 thuan (PS Co − PS No)
    2. Tru: Hang ban bi tra lai    = 5212 thuan (PS No − PS Co)
    3. = Doanh thu thuan
    4. Gia von hang ban            = 632 thuan
    5. = Loi nhuan gop ke toan
    6. Chi phi quan ly             = 642 thuan — co the tach dong
       (cung net: LoaiButToan + DAO_ tuong ung; loai KET_CHUYEN):
         6a. Luong (TRICH_LUONG ± DAO_TRICH_LUONG)
         6b. Dien/nuoc/thue/VP/cuoc (CHI_PHI ± DAO_CHI_PHI)
         6c. Khau hao (KHAU_HAO ± DAO_KHAU_HAO)
         6d. Xuat noi bo / khac
    7. Thu nhap khac               = 711 thuan (PS Co − PS No)
    8. = Loi nhuan ke toan truoc thue (mini)
       Chu thich man: CHUA xu ly thue TNDN. Khong goi "loi nhuan rong /
       LN sau thue".

  KHONG tru TRA_NCC. KHONG tru LECH_QUY vao DT (138/711 da nam dung cho).
  KHONG can nhanh "neu da ket chuyen thi lay PS Co 511" — da loai
  KET_CHUYEN / DAO_KET_CHUYEN khoi tap hop, nen so DT khong phu thuoc
  ky da khoa hay dang mo.

  Doi chieu kiem chung (khong de tinh KQKD):
