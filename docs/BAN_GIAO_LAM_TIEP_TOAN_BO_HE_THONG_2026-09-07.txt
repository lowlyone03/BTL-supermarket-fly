  Them -C vi ODBC Driver 18 tren mot so may bat SSL.

11. BA QUY TRINH + BA COT TIEN  (CAM PHA)
================================================================================

Day la luat xuong song. Sua so ma khong di dung buoc = vo do an.

----- COT A — HANG VA LAI GOP -----
  HD Hoan thanh: tru TonKho, snapshot DonGiaVon, GiaoDichKho Xuat.
  Doi tra Hoan thanh: giam DT/GV, khong sua tay ton.
  Lai gop = DT thuan - GV thuan.
  PHIEU THU KHONG CONG DOANH THU LAN HAI.
  Tien tra NCC KHONG tru vao lai gop (tru o P&L dieu hanh, muc rieng).

----- COT B — KET CA / PHIEU THU -----
  TienMatHeThong = TongTienMat - TongTienHoanMat.
  QR / The / CK KHONG vao ket.
  Mot ca DUNG MOT Phieu thu.
  Chenh lech = TienThucNop - TienMatHeThong; lech bat buoc LyDoChenhLech.
  Dong ca CHAN neu con HD Nhap hoac ThanhToan "Cho xac nhan".
  Ban 07/09: SoTienTheoHeThong / SoTienThucNop DUOC AM
  (ca chi hoan TM, hoan > thu). Van giu CK_PhieuThu_1 (lech phai co ly do).

----- COT C — CONG NO NCC -----
  Chi sinh CongNoPhaiTra khi doi chieu 3 ben KHOP
    (Don mua + Phieu nhap SoLuongChapNhan + Hoa don GTGT).
  Lap Phieu chi / QL duyet+giao quy / chi that bai: SoTienConLai KHONG DOI.
  Chi giam no khi KT ghi thanh toan THANH CONG.
  Khong tra truoc, khong tra tung phan, 1 cong no toi da 1 Phieu chi.

QUY TRINH 1 — MUA / NHAP
  TK dem that -> het/thieu moi de nghi (KHONG can QL duyet de nghi)
  -> MH lap PO -> QL duyet PO (lan duy nhat)
  -> NCC giao -> TK kiem, CHI SL chap nhan vao phieu nhap
  -> KT nhap HD GTGT -> doi chieu 3 chung tu
  -> Khop: sinh no 30-45 ngay. Lech: khong ghi no.
  Cam: nut "de nghi khai truong" (da bo), cong ton hang hu,
       giam no luc lap/duyet phieu chi.

QUY TRINH 2 — BAN / QUY CA
  QL cong bo lich -> TN dung ngay + trong gio (+ som ~10 phut)
  -> check-in / mo ca -> ban DU (khong ban chiu)
  -> HD hoan thanh tru ton + snapshot von
  -> dong ca -> KT lap Phieu thu (1 ca 1 phieu).
  Het ca / khong lich / chua check-in: API 403, khong chi khoa nut.

QUY TRINH 3 — LUONG (tat toan mung 10 thang sau)
  QL duyet cong (OT co phut) -> CHI KT bam Lap/tinh lai
  -> con cong cho duyet thi CHAN
  -> chi NV co phut cong duyet > 0; le 8h chi cho nguoi da di lam
  -> KT khoa ky -> lap phieu (moi NV TM HOAC CK)
  -> QL duyet tung nguoi hoac Duyet tat ca  (duyet CHUA tra)
  -> QL giao 1 CUC quy cho ke toan
  -> KT chi tung NV; CK bat buoc ma GD; fail thi lam lai cung phieu.
  Bang luong KHONG tru vao lai gop.
  P&L dieu hanh (man QL) moi tru luong DA KHOA + chi NCC thanh cong.

Cong thuc lai gop (khong doi so voi Chuong 6):
  DT thuan = DT HD hoan thanh - tien hoan da hoan thanh
  GV thuan = GV HD - GV hang tra nhap lai + GV hang giao doi
  Lai gop  = DT thuan - GV thuan
